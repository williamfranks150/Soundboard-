Help

Tap a button to play. Tap it again to stop and reset.

Hold a button to change its sound. Tap an empty button to open My Sounds.

In Saved sounds, tap play to preview and pause to stop. Tap Use to assign a sound.

Import audio from Files. MP3, M4A and WAV are supported. The filename becomes the sound name.

Tap Record to start recording, then Stop recording to finish. Tap Use sound to assign your import or recording.

After recording or importing, adjust Start and End to trim in seconds. Preview plays the selected section. Use sound saves that section. Reset restores the full selection before saving. For a saved recording, tap Trim to edit it; saving updates that sound wherever it is assigned.

Clear button removes its assignment. The sound stays in Saved sounds.

Imports, recordings and button assignments are saved on this device. Clearing website data deletes them.

Microphone recording requires permission and a secure HTTPS connection.
